Reciprocal Intercept



The three axes are separated by \(120^\circ\). A straight line intersecting these axes leads to the reciprocal equation \[ \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = \frac{1}{z}. \] The positive integer solutions are obtained by taking any positive divisor \(d\) of \(z^2\) and setting \[ x = z + d, \qquad y = z + \frac{z^2}{d}. \]
